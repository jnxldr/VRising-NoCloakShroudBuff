# Changelog

## 1.0.0
First release.
- Players who have killed Ben the Old Wanderer get the Shroud of the Forest buff in the Cursed Forest without wearing the cloak.
  It is removed when they leave the Cursed Forest. Wearing the cloak works as in the base game.
- Settings: `Enabled`, `CheckIntervalSeconds`, `LogChanges`; advanced: `Dump`, `DumpPattern`, `DumpIntervalSeconds`.
